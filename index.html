Engineering FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Thermal Engineering

A Thermal Applications Engineer analyzes heat transfer, fluid mechanics, psychrometrics, and thermodynamic cycles to design, optimize, and diagnose high-efficiency residential and commercial heating, cooling, and refrigeration systems.

Modern inverter-driven cold climate heat pumps maintain high coefficient of performance (COP) down to -15°F. Trey Durden designs custom hybrid dual-fuel systems that seamlessly blend electric heat pumps with modulating gas backup for 100% reliability.

Services include packaged rooftop unit (RTU) engineering, chilled water system optimization, building automation systems (BAS) commissioning, ventilation air quality compliance, and root-cause failure forensics.
